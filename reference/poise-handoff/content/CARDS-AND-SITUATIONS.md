# Kleos: 48 guidance cards and playable situations

Version 0.1.0. Authored material, not a trained or validated model. All characters and examples are fictional. These are additions to the existing catalogue, not replacements.

Each sharp verdict is conditional on the supplied mistake. Never apply it automatically to someone who handled the scene well.

## K01 / S01 — The internship interrogation

**Situation:** Dinner with your partner's friends. Someone turns every introduction into a recruiting screen.

**Character opens:** “So what do you actually do there?”

**Pressure:** They ask whether your parent got you the internship.

**User's job:** Answer honestly without making your entire introduction a defence.

**Guidance / retry:** Give one concrete task; acknowledge help if true; redirect only after answering.

**Mistake example:** “It's quite a prestigious programme actually.”

**Conditional Unfiltered read:** “You answered a question about your job with the company's ranking.”

**Branching:** Respond naturally to a good answer; ask one follow-up when ambiguous; challenge a weak answer once. Honour a clear boundary or exit. The scene is not required to end in approval.
